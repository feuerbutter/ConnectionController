ConnectionController

Privacy & Support · 31 October 2026

Your connections are processed locally

ConnectionController is a macOS utility from Weijun Li (Firebutter). It stores the app rules you choose, application paths and signing identities, permission bookmarks, reconnect preferences and your privacy acknowledgement on your Mac.

Its network extensions process application identities, network destinations, connection status and traffic in memory to apply Direct, VPN only or Block rules. Traffic is forwarded to its intended destination through your chosen interface. The app does not operate a VPN service and does not send traffic or browsing history to the developer.

No analytics or tracking

There is no developer-operated account, analytics service, advertising SDK or tracking. ConnectionController does not sell or share your personal data. It does not read your saved VPN passwords or add a developer-controlled VPN server. Your existing VPN provider, internet provider and destination services process network activity under their own policies.

Local settings and diagnostics

Your settings remain on your Mac. macOS may retain system diagnostics. ConnectionController writes local status and error messages for troubleshooting; these are not automatically uploaded to the developer. Removing a rule stops its inclusion in the next applied policy. Disable app rules before uninstalling if you want macOS to manage all routes again. Quit stops the reconnect controller; already applied network rules continue only with the choice disclosed by the Quit dialog. Launch at Login is optional.

What the app covers

The supported VPN setup is one enabled native IPSec profile configured in macOS. Direct uses an available physical Wi-Fi or Ethernet interface. Unlisted applications keep normal macOS routing. DNS to configured system resolvers follows macOS, including while an application is blocked. Localhost, independently launched helpers, VPN provider changes and unusual networking setups require separate consideration. ConnectionController does not promise anonymity or a permanent kill switch.

Help and contact

Add an application, choose its route, then select Apply app rules and follow the macOS extension prompts. Saved choices take effect after Apply. Use Disable rules to return applications to normal macOS routing. Open the menu-bar item to manage reconnect or optional login startup.

Contact zliverse@gmail.com for support or privacy questions. Include the app version, macOS version and a description of the problem. Do not send passwords or browsing traffic. If you choose to email us, the information you include is used to answer your request; it is not collected automatically by the app. Ask us to delete support correspondence when it is no longer needed, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Apple manages App Store distribution and any associated diagnostics or purchase information under Apple's own privacy policy.
